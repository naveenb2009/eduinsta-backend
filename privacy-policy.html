EduInsta Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

EduInsta ("we", "our", "the app") is an educational short-video app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled. EduInsta is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressAccount creation and sign-in, sent as a one-time verification code (OTP)
Name & usernameDisplayed on your profile and uploaded content
Profile photoDisplayed on your profile and reels so other users can recognize you
Videos you uploadTo publish your content in the app's feed; automatically screened for educational relevance before publishing
Comments you postDisplayed publicly under the reel you commented on, visible to other users
Subscription statusTo determine whether your account has an active ad-free Premium subscription
Advertising identifiersUsed by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads (not used if you have Premium)

We do not collect or store payment card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank details. Premium subscription payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; advertiser payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your payment credentials.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify it's really you via OTP
  • To show your uploaded videos to other users in the app
  • To screen uploaded videos for educational relevance before they're published
  • To determine your subscription status and whether ads should be shown
  • To serve relevant advertising, unless you're a Premium subscriber

Third parties we share data with

ServicePurpose
Google Play Billing / RevenueCatProcessing Premium subscription payments
RazorpayProcessing advertiser ad-campaign payments
Google AdMobServing in-app advertising
Google Gemini APIAutomated screening of uploaded videos for educational content
Cloudflare R2Storing uploaded video files
Neon (Postgres)Storing account and video metadata

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Children's privacy

EduInsta requires users to confirm they are 13 years of age or older at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will take steps to delete that account and associated data.

Data retention & deletion

We retain your account information and uploaded content for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the details below.

Security

OTP codes are hashed before storage and never transmitted back to the app. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing and Razorpay — we never receive or store your card, UPI, or bank details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
