Gyanora Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

Gyanora ("we", "our", "the app") is an educational short-video app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled. Gyanora is intended for users aged 13 and older.

We do not collect or store payment card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank details. Premium subscription payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; advertiser payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your payment credentials.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify it's really you via OTP
  • To show your uploaded videos to other users in the app
  • To screen uploaded videos for educational relevance before they're published
  • To show likes, comments, shares, followers and notifications
  • To review reports and remove content or accounts that break our rules
  • To send notifications you have chosen to receive, and sign-in alerts
  • To determine your subscription status and whether ads should be shown
  • To serve relevant advertising, unless you're a Premium subscriber

Children's privacy

Gyanora requires users to confirm they are 13 years of age or older at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will take steps to delete that account and associated data.

Content, reporting & safety

Every uploaded video is screened for educational relevance before it is published, and comments containing abusive words are refused. You can report any reel, comment or account from inside the app, block or mute accounts, and choose who can comment on your reels. Our team reviews reports and removes content or accounts that break our rules. Gyanora has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation: such content is removed, the account is deleted, and it is reported to the relevant authorities. To report a child-safety concern, contact us at the address below.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on our account-deletion page (/delete-account). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, uploaded videos, comments and replies, likes, follows, notifications, push tokens and reports you made about others.

Security

OTP codes are hashed before storage and never transmitted back to the app. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing and Razorpay — we never receive or store your card, UPI, or bank details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
